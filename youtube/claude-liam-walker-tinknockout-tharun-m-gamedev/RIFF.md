Voice: Liam (in for Bear), local Kokoro `am_onyx`. Register: Teardown — read the line, then point at what it
does on screen.

## Rules followed

* **Inspected before narrating.** The capture was watched as frame strips (`_qc/frames/fight-*.png`) and
  cross-read against `fight-inputs.jsonl` before B07/B09 were written. Narration names only events visible in
  the chosen interval (REWIND tag, HURT poses, pop-gun, EMPTY tag).
* **Corrected against the pixels.** B05 first said "red is the jab's hitbox"; the screenshots show no red box,
  so the line was cut and re-voiced (see FACTCHECK.md).
* **Posed is said out loud.** B03's engine panel and both B05 images are posed shots from `tools/shots.gd`;
  the narration says so.
* **Sound judgments are not made.** Claude cannot hear. The film never says a sound "fits" or "feels" right;
  it says the sounds were picked by signal analysis, and lets B10 play the game's audio with no voice over it
  so a human can judge.
* **No invented playtest.** The only human judgments in the film are Tharun's own, from his playtest recorded
  in TEST-REPORT.md (relayed facts: muted still readable, music fine alone, mix "full of metal sounds" from the
  tin SFX, characters very simple). All footage is scripted input and is never presented as a playtest.
* **One misconception, corrected once.** B01's hesitant-writer slip ("Sound decides the fight", corrected to "follows") is the idea
  B08, B11 and B12 then dismantle.
